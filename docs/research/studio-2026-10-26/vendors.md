# Customer research: edtech vendors as buyers of a Swedish school-contract data studio

Research date: 2026-10-26. Scope: vendors selling digital tools to Swedish (and Nordic) schools, especially new entrants. Sources are linked inline. "Vendor claim" means the figure comes from the company's own marketing.

---

## 0. Bottom line

- Swedish school buying is split three ways: a **central kommun licence** (förvaltning/IT/läromedelsgrupp), **rektor buying out of the school's own budget**, and **calls on a reseller framework** (Adda via Skolon / Läromedia / Atea). A vendor can't see from outside which route a kommun uses. Our dataset shows it per avtal (direct vs reseller/ramavtal).
- **The biggest sales trigger in years is 3 months away.** Adda's *Digitala läromedel 2022* framework expires **31 Jan 2027**. Its successor, *Digitala läromedel 2026*, starts **1 Feb 2027** and runs 4 years. Skolon is confirmed as one of three suppliers; the other two are not public yet in the sources I found. About 70% of kommuner are connected, and each has to re-connect and re-call.
- **Direktupphandlingsgräns is 700 000 kr (LOU) in 2026**, a fixed amount set in the law. A new vendor can sell to a kommun without a tender if the total contract value stays under it. A studio that shows "typical contract value per kommun size" tells a vendor right away whether a deal can be bought direct.
- Existing Swedish procurement-intel tools (Tendium, Mercell/Opic, Pabliq, e-Avrop, TendSign) are built on **tender notices and award data**. Most edtech buying never produces a tender (direct buys, framework call-offs, rektor purchases), so these tools can't see it. **Nobody sells the signed contracts with unit prices, segment coverage and renewal dates.** That is our gap.
- The closest analogues are US (GovSpend, Burbio, Starbridge: FOIA-sourced purchase orders and check registers) and UK (Tussell: contracts + invoice spend + framework call-offs, **£11,400 per licence per year** list price). They sell **expiry-based pipeline, competitor footprint, and price benchmarks**, the same three things our dataset is strongest at.

## 1. How Swedish public school buying works for digital tools

### Who decides
- **Huvudman = kommun** (or the friskola owner). The kommun is responsible for school digital infrastructure ([OECD country review](https://oecd.org/en/publications/country-digital-education-ecosystems-and-governance_906134d4-en/full-report/component-30.html)). Formal decisions to join a framework are taken by the **nämnd** (e.g. barn- och grundskolenämnden), and the utbildningschef signs ([Täby tjänsteutlåtande 2023](https://doc.taby.se/handlingar/Barn-%20och%20grundskolen%C3%A4mnden/2023/2023-02-08/Handlingar/04.%20Tj%C3%A4nsteutl%C3%A5tande%20digitala%20l%C3%A4romedel.pdf)).
- **Central licences**: many kommuner run a central group that decides on kommun-wide licences. In Sundsvall teachers submit requests through an e-service, the läromedelsgrupp weighs them against need and budget, and the answer comes **at the end of the läsår** ([Sundsvall](https://www.gymnasium.sundsvall.se/kommun/utbildning-och-forskola/kvalitet-och-utveckling/pedagog-sundsvall/digitala-larresurser)).
- **Rektor**: holds the school budget and can decide how it is spent, including läromedel ([Härryda nämndhandling](https://www.harryda.se/download/18.1b1fe85b1885185e2ba4669/1685358794938/11.pdf)). In Göteborg 2023 the politicians argued over whether to steer rektors' budgets toward läromedel, which shows the money normally sits with the rektor ([Göteborg yrkande](https://www4.goteborg.se/prod/intraservice/namndhandlingar/samrumportal.nsf/88A22F094FA15628C1258BA10030CF7E/$File/16.%20Yrkande%20fran%20M%20D%20och%20L%20med%20sarskilt%20yttrande%20fran%20KD%20om%20Laromedelsinkop%20for%202024%20som%20mojliggor%20avrop%20av%20statsbidrag.pdf)).
- Practical upshot for a vendor: **LMS, skoladministration and prov tools are usually central kommun decisions** (one contract, long term). **Läromedel and stödverktyg** are often rektor-level purchases made through a central channel (Skolon/Läromedia). Our category split matches this, and the studio should show "central vs per-school" per kommun where the avtal reveals it.

### Budgets
- Läromedel spend is small and very uneven: **859 kr per elev in 2021** on average (733 kr in 2020), about 0.7% of cost per elev. **Göteborg 2,049 kr, Grästorp 63 kr** ([Läromedelsföretagen barometer, via Skaraborgsnyheter](https://skaraborgsnyheter.se/?p=21734); [TV4](https://www.tv4.se/artikel/DZ5rgGroS2cZswfIkljRL/stora-skillnader-i-satsningar-pa-laeromedel-i-landet-aer-tufft)). No newer barometer figure was published after 2021 ([Riksdag debate 2024](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/_hb01ubu6/)).
- SKR says läromedel cost is hard to follow up because Skolverket's "lärverktyg" line also contains computers, consumables and software ([SKR remissvar](https://www.regeringen.se/contentassets/a8a49f71a17b45efb53b54862ffd8916/sveriges-kommuner-och-regioner.pdf)). **There is no public number for digital tool spend per kommun.** Our contract values are the only bottom-up source.
- **Statsbidrag för läroböcker och lärarhandledningar** (SFS 2023:86, amended 2025:88): a yearly frame per huvudman based on its share of elever (3-year average), paid per **calendar year**, and the books must be bought in that year ([lagen.nu](https://lagen.nu/2023:86/konsolidering/2024:267); [SFS 2023:86](https://svenskforfattningssamling.se/sites/default/files/sfs/2023-03/SFS2023-86.pdf)). 533 mnkr was paid out for 2023 ([SVT](https://www.svt.se/nyheter/inrikes/bidragsmissen-svenska-elever-gick-miste-om-larobocker-for-over-36-miljoner-kronor-forra-aret)). An extra 500 mnkr is planned for 2028 ([Regeringen budget 2026](https://www.regeringen.se/regeringens-politik/utbildningsdepartementets-samlade-budgetsatsningar/utbildningsdepartementets-samlade-budgetsatsningar-2026/)). Whether a given digital product qualifies needs checking per product; the grant pushes books, not tools. Swedish Edtech Industry has publicly objected to the print bias ([press release](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/se/swedish-edtech-industry/pressreleases/swedish-edtech-i-debattutspel-baade-den-tryckta-laeroboken-och-det-digitala-laeromedlet-behoevs-3217473)).

### Direktupphandlingsgräns
- **700 000 kr** for goods and services under LOU from 1 Jan 2026, fixed in the law. About 8.5 mnkr for social and other special services ([Konkurrensverket, Analys i korthet 2026:1](https://www.konkurrensverket.se/globalassets/dokument/informationsmaterial/rapporter-och-broschyrer/analys-i-korthet/analys-i-korthet_2026-1.pdf); [Mercell on 2026 thresholds](https://info.mercell.com/en/blog/new-procurement-thresholds-take-effect-on-1-january-2026/)). The value is the whole contract (all years plus options) and is summed over the kommun's same-kind purchases. A 4-year deal at 175 000 kr/year is at the limit.
- Konkurrensverket reviewed kommun direktupphandling guidelines in June 2026 (same report). Many kommuner have stricter internal rules (e.g. extra quotes required above a lower amount), see [Ragunda riktlinjer](https://ragunda.se/download/18.72d3c53d19e48ccc9c1472b/1779783735091/Riktlinjer%20f%C3%B6r%20upphandling%20inklusive%20direktupphandling.pdf).

### Adda ramavtal Digitala läromedel 2022 and successor
- **2022**: Delområde A (digital) Atea, LäroMedia Bokhandel Örebro, Skolon. Delområde B (combined print+digital) LäroMedia only. Call-off by **fördelningsnyckel** (the supplier that best meets the need on: technical fit, environment, pedagogical design, delivery time, price). Runs 4 years, **latest end 31 Jan 2027**. Täby estimated about **5 mnkr over the period** and guaranteed no volume ([Täby tjänsteutlåtande](https://doc.taby.se/handlingar/Barn-%20och%20grundskolen%C3%A4mnden/2023/2023-02-08/Handlingar/04.%20Tj%C3%A4nsteutl%C3%A5tande%20digitala%20l%C3%A4romedel.pdf); [Skolon release 2022](https://storage.mfn.se/c/aHR0cHM6Ly9tYi5jaXNpb24uY29tL1B1YmxpYy8yMTQ1Ny8zNjU2Njc5L2FlNmEzZmEzZWNiZjAzNjIucGRm/skolon-tilldelas-kontrakt-i-svensk-ramavtalsupphandling-fo-r-inko-p-av-digitala-la-romedel-221027.pdf)). Adda also publishes a discount-rate sheet per supplier ([rabattsatser](https://adda.se/globalassets/1-upphandling-ramavtal/filer-och-block/laromedel-2022/digitala/rabattsatser-per-leverantor.pdf)).
- Print and digital were split into separate frameworks for the first time in 2022 ([Nordic EdTech News #76](https://nordicedtech.substack.com/p/nordic-edtech-news-76-2022-10-31)).
- **Successor "Digitala läromedel 2026"**: covers förskola, grundskola, gymnasium, **SFI and vuxenutbildning** (wider than 2022) ([TED notice 329929-2026](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/329929-2026/pdf)). Three suppliers, 4 years from **1 Feb 2027**; Skolon confirmed on 8 Sep 2026 ([Placera/Skolon](https://www.placera.se/telegram/skolon-skolon-far-nytt-fyraarigt-ramavtal-med-adda-20260908); [Inderes](https://www.inderes.se/releases/skolon-far-fornyat-fortroende-fran-adda-tilldelas-nytt-ramavtal-for-digitala-laromedel)). The other two winners were not in the sources found; check Adda's site. Adda's 2026 procurement plan: [PDF](https://www.adda.se/globalassets/2.-upphandling-ramavtal/8.-upphandlingsplan-2026/adda_upphandlingsplan_2026_a4_webb_ny.pdf).
- For a vendor this matters because **being listed with the reseller(s) a kommun calls off from is the route to market** for läromedel and stödverktyg. A studio view "which kommuner call off via which reseller, and which reseller carries my product" is directly actionable in Q4 2026 to Q1 2027.

### Skolon and Läromedia
- **Skolon**: platform plus marketplace, 5,500+ tools and läromedel from about 200 suppliers, 1M+ paying users (vendor claim) ([Skolon releases](https://www.inderes.se/releases/skolon-far-tilldelning-i-upphandling-av-malmo-stad-omfattar-upp-till-50-000-anvandare)). Kommuner tell staff that all digital läromedel purchases must go through Skolon so purchases stay LOU-compliant; licences appear in "Min samling" when the purchase completes ([Skolon mail template](https://support.skolon.com/sv/kb/articles/pdf/information-till-dig-som-k-per-in-digitala-l-romedel-mailmall)). Supplier commission terms are not public.
- **Läromedia**: dominant reseller for physical and digital läromedel; Växjö reports **90-95% of its läromedel purchases** went through Läromedia ([Växjö interpellationssvar 2023](https://www.boplats.vaxjo.se/download/18.292d2fda18b37c2859a1069/1697452970624/Interpellationssvar%20kommunfullm%C3%A4ktige%202023-10-17.pdf)).
- Consequence: for reseller-bought products, **the kommun's contract is with the reseller, not the publisher**. A vendor can't see its own end-customer footprint from its own CRM if it sells through Skolon/Läromedia. Our per-avtal "via reseller" flag plus product lines is exactly that view.

### Contract length and timing
- Framework agreements: 4 years (Adda). Kommun-level LMS/admin contracts: typically 2-4 years plus 1-2 option years (verify on our own data: we have periods for 82% of avtal, so this can be measured rather than assumed).
- Läromedel licences: often per **läsår** (a Finnish example states "licenstid 1 läsår", [Sets](https://laromedel.sets.fi/bok/hbl-junior-skola-lasar/)). I found no source for a national order deadline; Sundsvall decides at läsår end. Working assumption to verify in our data: decisions in spring (Mar-Jun), start in August, plus a **Q4 calendar-year rush** to use up budget and the statsbidrag (which must be spent within the calendar year).
- Nordic note: Skolon also wins Norwegian kommun tenders (Fredrikstad/Hvaler 10,500 licences, about NOK 5M/year; Haugalandet 15,000 licences, about NOK 10M/year) ([Skolon Norway](https://storage.mfn.se/c/aHR0cHM6Ly9tYi5jaXNpb24uY29tL1B1YmxpYy8yMTQ1Ny8zNjE4NDIxL2FlMDU2M2YzMDkwMThlZjYucGRm/skolon-tilldelas-kontrakt-i-norsk-upphandling-fo-r-fredrikstad-och-hvaler-kommun.pdf)). In Denmark, Copenhagen found **10-30% price differences per licence** across its own schools, and Viborg saved 26% by putting subjects out to one supplier through SKI's dynamic purchasing system ([Københavns Kommune bilag](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/39c2e60b-27df-46d6-9553-2710d1ebda0a/55cc28ee-c660-4cff-bb36-7ea4fd13e6ce-bilag-2.pdf)). That is direct evidence that per-licence prices vary a lot between buyers, and that nobody sees it.

## 2. What market intelligence vendors need and lack

### Evidence of the gap
- US research has found for years that edtech vendors have "only a vague sense of district officials' specific buying needs", and that slow, complex procurement "tends to exclude new and smaller ed-tech companies" while buyers "stick with companies with an established track record" ([EdWeek 2014 study](https://edweek.org/leadership/ed-tech-vendors-often-in-dark-on-district-needs-study-shows/2014/11); [EdWeek Market Brief on CRPE report](https://marketbrief.edweek.org/marketplace-k-12/ed-tech_purchasing_bottlenecks_bureaucracy_probed_in_report/)).
- Europe: the European Edtech Map lists 1,200+ companies; most are small and only about half sell outside their home market ([Swedish Edtech Industry, Jan 2023](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/se/swedish-edtech-industry/pressreleases/ny-rapport-om-europeisk-edtech-smaa-foeretag-paa-lokala-marknader-3227648)). Swedish Edtech Industry has about **100 member companies** ([consultation response](https://www.regeringen.se/contentassets/160b0cadef544cf7867ddf28e3ced268/swedish-edtech-industri.pdf); [new members](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/se/swedish-edtech-industry/pressreleases/tre-nya-medlemmar-i-swedish-edtech-ai-mognad-skoladministration-och-barns-naetsaekerhet-3432090)). Its CEO chairs the European Edtech Alliance (about 26 associations, 23 countries). It is a natural distribution partner.
- Market size: no authoritative figure. An AI-generated report puts Swedish formal-education edtech at 2.5-3 bn SEK ([SourceReady](https://www.sourceready.com/report/detail/sweden-edtech-market-report-2026), unreliable). HolonIQ's Nordic-Baltic EdTech 50 shows K-12 falling to 44% of the cohort in 2025 from 64% in 2020 ([HolonIQ](https://www.holoniq.com/notes/2025-nordic-baltic-edtech-50)), meaning vendors are moving away from K-12, partly because municipal budgets are tight ([KS Norway on budget pressure](https://www.ks.no/fagomrader/okonomi/statsbudsjettet-2025/--frykter-svekket-okonomi-vil-gi-darligere-digital-skolehverdag/)). **A bottom-up market size per category from real avtal would be new.**
- Consolidation is ongoing, e.g. Unikum (100+ kommuner) bought StudyBee in 2025 ([BeBeez](https://bebeez.eu/2025/06/05/swedish-edtech-merger-brings-assessment-and-analytics-under-one-roof/)). Incumbent footprint per kommun is what acquirers and challengers both want.

### What existing Swedish procurement-intel tools offer
| Tool | What it covers | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Tendium** | Tender search/AI bid help; "Analytics & Insights": which buyers competitors sell to, what they bid, what they invoice; expiring contracts with expiry dates, extensions, value. 1,400+ customers, 1.8M European tenders (vendor claims) ([Tendium analytics](https://tendium.ai/analytics-insights/); [Tendium intelligence](https://tendium.ai/en/tendium-intelligence/); [TrustRadius](https://www.trustradius.com/products/tendium/details)) | From about €125/month entry; Core/Scale quoted ([CheckThat listing](https://checkthat.ai/brands/tendium)) |
| **Mercell (incl. Opic, TendSign)** | Visma sold Opic and TendSign to Mercell. Opic = monitoring of procurement info, competitors, prospects and "their outgoing agreements"; shows current supplier, price level and evaluation criteria of the previous tender ([Visma release](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/visma/pressreleases/visma-to-divest-two-of-its-product-areas-within-procurement-software-3122211); [Driva Eget, sponsored](https://driva-eget.se/artiklar/vinna-offentliga-upphandlingar-med-mercell-opic)) | Not public |
| **Pabliq, e-Avrop, Byggfakta Tender** | Tender notification and bid platforms | Not public / freemium |

**The structural limit**: all of these are built on published tenders and awards (plus, for Tendium, some invoice data). A direct purchase under 700 000 kr, a call-off on the Adda framework, or a rektor buying in Skolon produces no notice. In our dataset a large share of läromedel and stödverktyg avtal are exactly these. They also don't extract **unit prices, pricing model or grade segments** from the contract text.

### Analogues abroad: what they show and what customers pay for
- **Tussell (UK)**: contracts + £8trn invoice spend + 170k framework call-offs matched to frameworks/lots; Competitor Tracker shows all of a competitor's soon-to-expire contracts; pipeline from expiries + pre-tender signals from minutes and strategies; buyer/supplier profiles; alerts; market sizing; spend chart by year/quarter/month; named account manager. **£11,400 per licence per year** ([G-Cloud 14](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-14/services/430518247842905); [Competitor Tracker](https://www.tussell.com/products/competitor-tracker); [Tussell vs competitors](https://www.tussell.com/competitors); [2022 updates](https://tussell.com/insights/getting-the-bigger-picture-tussell-platform-upgrades-updates-so-far-in-2022)). A customer testimonial: alerts on contracts nearing renewal, "who contracts sit with", the range of suppliers a buyer uses ([Coventry Uni](https://www.tussell.com/hubfs/Coventry%20Uni%20Testimonial%20-%202024_03_27.pdf?hsLang=en)).
- **GovSpend (US)**: purchase orders from tens of thousands of public-records requests per month (exactly our method, at scale): PO number, date, line items, quantities, **prices**, vendor; K-12 coverage of 91 of the top 100 districts; board meetings; pitches "historical pricing data and current vendor contracts" to write proposals ([GovSpend data overview](https://support.govspend.com/data-overview-spending-pos); [GovSpend K-12](https://govspend.com/?p=15291)).
- **Burbio (US)**: K-12 check registers for thousands of districts searchable by vendor and category, board minutes, budgets; delivered as **district profiles** and market reports, pushed into CRM; customers are curriculum, tech and software suppliers ([Burbio about](https://about.burbio.com/about-us); [Neudata](https://www.neudata.co/1/vendors/burbio)). Domo partnership adds dashboards + AI Q&A over district documents ([Domo](https://domo-webflow.domo.com/news/press/domo-and-burbio-to-launch-k-12-education-data-intelligence-tool)).
- **Starbridge (US)**: "buying signals" (board minutes, budgets, grants, **contract expirations**), competitor contracts obtained via FOIA automation, current contract + pricing + expiration date per district, account scoring, CRM sync, AI outreach drafts (vendor claims) ([Starbridge vs Burbio](https://starbridge.ai/blog/starbridge-vs-burbio); [EdTech landing](https://starbridge.ai/lp/ai-gtm-platform-edtech); [GovSpend alternatives](https://starbridge.ai/blog/govspend-alternatives)).
- **EdWeek Market Brief (US)**: research/news + surveys of K-12 decision-makers + biweekly purchasing alerts; organisation-wide membership, quote only ([Market Brief](https://marketbrief.edweek.org/subscribe-edbiz-update-newsletter-powered-edweek-market-brief/); [EdWeek group pricing](https://edweek.org/group-subscriptions/pricing)).
- **LearnPlatform by Instructure (US)**: EdTech Top 40, usage-based ranking; districts access on average 2,739 distinct tools a year ([eSchool News](https://www.eschoolnews.com/live-iste-2024/2024/07/02/iste-live-news-instructure-releases-the-edtech-top-40/)). Shows that "who is used where" rankings are a marketing asset vendors pay attention to (and cite in their own PR, e.g. [Kahoot](https://kahoot.com/blog/2024/06/26/edtech-top-40/)).

**What customers pay for, consistently across these tools:** (1) pipeline from **expiry dates** of competitors' contracts, (2) **competitor footprint** per buyer, (3) **historical pricing** to set and defend a bid, (4) **buyer profiles** with who-decides and contact, (5) alerts and CRM export. Pre-tender "signals" from minutes are the next tier. Pricing is a per-seat annual licence in the £5k-15k range (Tussell) with enterprise quotes above.

### Needs mapped to our data
- **Market size per category**: sum of annual value by category, scaled to 290 kommuner by elevantal (we have 185 kommuner; 62% of values known, so show coverage).
- **Price benchmarks**: kr per elev per segment and per pricing model. Rare in any public source. Copenhagen's 10-30% spread shows the need.
- **Competitor footprint**: vendor x kommun matrix, product-level.
- **Renewal windows**: end dates and option years for 82% of avtal; 6-12 months before end is the sales trigger.
- **Channel**: direct vs Skolon/Läromedia/Atea/Adda per avtal.
- **White spots**: kommuner with no avtal in a category, or only a weak incumbent.

## 4. Design and UX prior art

Sources: product pages and help docs of Tussell, Dealroom, Crunchbase, Similarweb, Pitchbook. Layout descriptions are partly from my own knowledge of these products; where no public source describes the screen, I say so. Neither Tussell nor Crunchbase publishes screen-level documentation that I could find.

### Page types that recur
1. **Entity page for a company (vendor)**: header with name, category tags, key numbers (kommuner, total annual value, avtal count, share of category); tabs for Customers, Products, Contracts, Pricing, Similar companies. Dealroom profiles carry funding, team, news, competitors and similar companies ([Dealroom guide](https://www.ellty.com/blog/what-is-dealroom)); Tussell has supplier profiles with spend and framework usage ([G-Cloud](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-14/services/430518247842905)).
2. **Entity page for a buyer (kommun)**: Tussell buyer profile = spend over time (toggle year/quarter/month), top suppliers, contracts, frameworks used, contacts ([Tussell 2022 updates](https://tussell.com/insights/getting-the-bigger-picture-tussell-platform-upgrades-updates-so-far-in-2022)). Burbio sells its data as district profiles ([Neudata](https://www.neudata.co/1/vendors/burbio)).
3. **Category / market page**: largest suppliers, latest activity, most used routes to market ([Tussell category management](https://www.tussell.com/gov/solutions/procurement-category-management)). Similarweb's "industry benchmark" is the same idea: you against the category over time ([TrustRadius review](https://trustradius.com/reviews/similarweb-pro-2023-04-04-08-17-38)).
4. **Comparison**: 2-5 entities side by side, same metrics, same scale (Similarweb compare; Crunchbase/Pitchbook comparable tables).
5. **Lists with filters (the screener)**: a table with a left-side filter rail, sortable columns, chosen columns, save as list, export. Crunchbase users value "fast search, useful filters, list building" and complain when exports are plan-gated ([RFP.wiki comparison](https://www.rfp.wiki/vendors/dealroom/crunchbase)).
6. **Saved searches and alerts**: Dealroom's Activity Center collects saved searches, lists and alerts in one place; saved searches turn into alerts when new matching entities appear ([Dealroom release notes](https://knowledge.dealroom.co/knowledge/product-release-updates?hsLang=en)). Tussell: alerts on expiries and new tenders; Starbridge/Burbio: keyword and competitor trackers.
7. **Pipeline / timeline**: Tussell builds pipeline from expiring contracts plus pre-tender signals ([Tussell](https://www.tussell.com/competitors)). For us: a renewal calendar.
8. **Integrations**: CSV/API and CRM sync are table stakes for sales teams (Tussell, Burbio, Starbridge all offer them).

### Patterns that make a page something you can act on
- **Lead with the answer number and its delta**, then the evidence. A vendor page opens with "38 kommuner, 4.2 mnkr/yr, 11 expiring within 12 months", not a table.
- **Every number is a link to its rows.** Click "11 expiring" and get the 11 avtal, each linked to the PDF excerpt. This is our edge: the source document is the proof, which tender tools can't offer.
- **Time is a first-class axis.** Gantt of contracts per kommun; "expiring in" buckets (0-6, 6-12, 12-18 months); the next decision date per buyer.
- **"Me vs them" framing.** Let a vendor claim its company; then every page shows its own position (price marker on the benchmark, own kommuner highlighted on the map, gap list = kommuner where a competitor sits and they don't).
- **Saved list -> alert -> export** on every list. A screen without a "watch this" button is a report, not a tool.
- **Coverage shown next to every aggregate**: "based on 1,676 avtal from 185 of 290 kommuner; value known for 62%". This also fits Mediagraf's no-over-claiming rule. Separate "no avtal" from "not asked / not delivered".
- **Map + list together** for buyers (kommun geography matters to regional sellers), but the list is the primary object.
- **Similar entities** module (lookalike kommuner, similar vendors), as Dealroom/Crunchbase do, drives the next click.
- **Small multiples over dual axes** for comparisons; consistent scales across compared entities.

### Suggested first studio screens (from the above)
1. **Renewal calendar** (question 1, 13): filter by category/segment/region, sorted by end date, save + alert.
2. **Vendor page** (2, 9, 10): footprint, products, segments, expiries, price position.
3. **Kommun page** (8, 15): timeline of all edtech avtal, channel, spend by category.
4. **Category page** (3, 6, 10): market value with coverage, vendor share, price benchmark per segment.
5. **Gap list** (7, 12): kommuner without category X, ranked by size (needs elevantal).

### Pricing reference points
- Tussell £11,400/licence/year; Tendium from about €125/month; US tools quote-only, enterprise. A Swedish edtech new entrant has a small budget; a tiered model (free category page teaser, paid vendor seat, enterprise with API/CRM) mirrors the analogues.
